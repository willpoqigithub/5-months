I promise to love you, to take care of you, and to protect what we have. I will not neglect you. I will treat you with the respect, gentleness, and honesty you deserve — the kind of treatment that makes you feel safe and valued.

I will keep showing you how important you are to me and how deeply I care. I will not get tired of reminding you. And when I make mistakes — because I know I will — I promise to humble myself, admit where I went wrong, and sincerely apologize. I never want my pride to cost me you.

With God

May the Lord continue to guide this relationship. May He keep it strong, pure, and grounded in patience and understanding. I pray that God remains at the center of everything we build together — in the quiet moments, in the hard ones, and in all the days still ahead of us.

For our future

I hope that one day we both become successful in the ways that matter most. I hope every quiet dream we’ve talked about finds its way into reality. I hope all the “soons” we’ve been holding onto eventually become “finally.”

Until that day comes, I will keep choosing this. I will keep choosing you — in the small ways and in the lasting ones.
